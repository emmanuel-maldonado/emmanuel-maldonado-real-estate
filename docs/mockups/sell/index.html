How it works

Three simple steps to a realistic number.

Online estimates cannot see your kitchen, your roof, or the street you live on. A comparable-sales analysis looks at the homes buyers will actually compare yours to.

STEP 01

Tell me about your home

STEP 02

You get a suggested price range and a plain-language explanation, in person, by phone, or by video, in English or Spanish.

Good to know: this free market analysis is an opinion of value based on comparable sales. It is not an appraisal and should not be used in place of one for lending or legal purposes.

Seller questions

Common questions about selling.

What is my home worth in Virginia Beach?

Your home's value depends on recent comparable sales nearby, its condition, size, layout, updates, and how many similar homes buyers can choose from right now. Online estimates are a starting point, but they cannot see inside your home.

A comparable-sales market analysis compares your home with similar homes that recently sold near you to estimate a realistic price range. It is free, and it is not an appraisal. For context, the median recorded price of a Virginia Beach home sale over the past 12 months was $403,000.Source: City of Virginia Beach Real Estate Assessor public sales records, 6,497 residential sales recorded Sep 29, 2025 – Sep 28, 2026 (single-family, townhouse, condo, duplex), excluding non-market transfers. Retrieved Oct 29, 2026. For comparison, Redfin reports $429,795 for the 3 months ending Sep 2026.

How long does it take to sell a house?

Timing depends on price, condition, location, the season, and local inventory. Well-prepared homes priced in line with recent sales tend to attract interest sooner. After you accept an offer, the closing date follows the contract, often based on the buyer's financing.

Lately, Virginia Beach homes have gone under contract in a median of about 28 days, and Hampton Roads homes overall in 30 days.Sources: Redfin Virginia Beach housing market, data through Sep 2026 (retrieved Oct 29, 2026); REIN September 2026 market release, published Oct 9, 2026.

What does it cost to sell a house in Virginia?

Common costs include brokerage compensation (which is negotiable), settlement and closing fees, Virginia grantor's tax and other recording costs, payoff of any mortgage or liens, prorated property taxes, and any repairs or credits you agree to with the buyer. Before you list, I will walk you through an estimated net proceeds sheet so there are no surprises.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

Not always. Some sellers make small repairs and touch-ups to appeal to more buyers. Others sell as-is and price the home to reflect its condition. The right choice depends on the repair, what it costs, and how it affects value and buyer financing. We can decide together which projects are worth it.
